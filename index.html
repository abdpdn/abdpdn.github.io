Ashirbad Padhan

Postdoctoral Researcher in Theoretical Condensed Matter Physics
CNRS–Laboratoire de Physique Théorique, Toulouse, France

I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist specializing in the numerical study of strongly correlated quantum many-body systems. My research focuses on quantum phase transitions, topological phases, many-body localization, and non-Hermitian quantum matter. I primarily use large-scale numerical techniques, including density-matrix renormalization group and matrix-product-state methods, exact diagonalization, and cluster mean-field theory, to investigate the interplay among interactions, disorder, topology, and quantum entanglement in low-dimensional lattice systems.
